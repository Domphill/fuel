# Fuel

A calorie, protein and training tracker that runs on your phone. Scan barcodes, log meals, track workouts and weight.
Your diary is stored only on your device. Barcode lookups send just the barcode number to Open Food Facts.

Food data: McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset 2021 (Open Government Licence v3.0). Product data: Open Food Facts (ODbL). Barcode reading: ZXing (Apache 2.0).
